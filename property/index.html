REGISTRATION SERVICES

Sale Deed Registration

Drafting, stamp duty guidance, Kaveri 2.0 online submission and registration of the sale deed at the jurisdictional Sub-Registrar Office.

AvailableDepartment of Stamps and Registration, Government of Karnataka
PROPERTY VERIFICATION

Encumbrance Certificate (EC)

Apply for and read an Encumbrance Certificate - the registered transaction history of a property for a chosen period.

AvailableDepartment of Stamps and Registration
PROPERTY VERIFICATION

Title Verification

Systematic verification of the title chain, parent documents, Khatha, tax, revenue and approval records before you transact.

AvailableRecords of the Sub-Registrar, municipal authority, Revenue Department and planning authority as applicable
LEGAL SERVICES

Property Legal Opinion

A written legal opinion on title, transactions or disputes, issued by an independent qualified property lawyer.

AvailableIndependent qualified advocate engaged for the opinion
RENT PROPERTY

Rental Agreement

Residential rental agreements with correct stamping, clear deposit, lock-in, maintenance and exit clauses.

AvailablePrivate instrument; stamping under the Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957 and registration where the law requires
POWER OF ATTORNEY

General Power of Attorney (Property)

Drafting and execution of a General Power of Attorney for property management, with honest limits on what a GPA can and cannot do.

AvailablePrivate instrument; adjudication, stamping and registration as applicable
PROPERTY TAX & KHATHA

Khatha Transfer

Transfer the municipal Khatha to the new owner's name after a sale, gift or inheritance - through the current Bengaluru municipal system.

AvailableGreater Bengaluru Authority / BBMP
PROPERTY TAX & KHATHA

Property Tax Payment Assistance

Pay Bengaluru property tax correctly - account identification, arrears check, payment on the official portal and receipt reconciliation.

AvailableGreater Bengaluru Authority / BBMP
UTILITY TRANSFERS

BESCOM Name Transfer (Electricity)

Transfer the BESCOM electricity account to the new owner's name after a property transfer, using BESCOM's official name-change process.

AvailableBangalore Electricity Supply Company (BESCOM)
UTILITY TRANSFERS

BWSSB Name Transfer (Water and Sewerage)

Transfer the BWSSB water and sewerage account to the new owner's name through the official transfer process.

AvailableBangalore Water Supply and Sewerage Board (BWSSB)
PROPERTY CONVERSION & APPROVALS

Agricultural Land Conversion (DC Conversion)

Conversion of agricultural land to non-agricultural use through the Deputy Commissioner's office, with order verification.

AvailableDeputy Commissioner of the district
PROPERTY VERIFICATION

Karnataka RERA Project Verification

Verify a project's and promoter's Karnataka RERA registration, declared timelines and status on the official portal.

AvailableKarnataka Real Estate Regulatory Authority (K-RERA)
