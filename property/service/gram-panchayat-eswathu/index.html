Gram Panchayat Khatha and e-Swathu Records

Form 9, Form 11A/11B and e-Swathu records for Gram Panchayat properties, with Panchayat tax and mutation support.

Gram Panchayat and Rural Property Servicese-Swathu documentationAvailable - Phase 1

What is this service?

The rural property record system for Gram Panchayat areas.

Properties in Gram Panchayat areas are recorded in the e-Swathu system, which issues Form 9 and Form 11A/11B records used for taxation and transactions. Panchayat-area property work - records, tax, mutation, licences - runs through the Panchayat and the e-Swathu portal, not the city municipal system.

Why and when you need it

Why
Form 9 and Form 11 records are the first documents asked for in any Panchayat-area transaction.
Who needs it
Owners, buyers and heirs of properties in Gram Panchayat limits.
When
For any transaction, tax payment or record update in a Panchayat area.

Required documents

  • Title documents of the property
  • Existing Form 9 / Form 11 records, if any
  • Identity proof of the owner
  • Survey and site details

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Confirm the property's Panchayat jurisdiction
  2. Retrieve existing e-Swathu records for the property
  3. Apply for the record, update or certificate needed
  4. Pay Panchayat tax dues where pending
  5. Verify the issued record

Legal requirements
  • Karnataka Panchayat Raj Act and related rules as applicable
Common rejection reasons
  • Property not entered in e-Swathu
  • Title records not matching the Panchayat record
  • Tax dues pending
Important cautions
Do not assume a Panchayat-area property falls under BBMP or BDA - jurisdiction decides everything
Form 9 and Form 11 records support but do not by themselves prove title
Conversions and layout approvals in Panchayat areas have their own route - check before transacting

FAQs

What are Form 9 and Form 11?
e-Swathu records for Panchayat properties - Form 9 for properties assessed to tax and Form 11A/11B variants as applicable. They are the standard records asked for in transactions.
How do I check a Panchayat property record?
Search the property on the official e-Swathu portal.
Is Panchayat Khatha the same as BBMP Khatha?
No - they are records of different authorities for different jurisdictions.
