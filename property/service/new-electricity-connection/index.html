New BESCOM Electricity Connection

New domestic or commercial electricity connections with BESCOM - application, documents, inspection and meter installation.

Electricity Connection and TransferNew electricity connectionAvailable - Phase 1

What is this service?

A fresh electricity service connection for a property.

A new electricity connection requires an application to BESCOM with ownership and wiring-related documents, followed by inspection and meter installation. The process and charges are published by BESCOM for each consumer category.

Why and when you need it

Why
Occupation, construction and commercial activity all depend on a lawful connection.
Who needs it
Owners of newly constructed or newly acquired properties without a connection.
When
Before occupation or use of the property.

Required documents

  • Ownership or occupancy proof
  • Identity proof of the applicant
  • Approved building plan where applicable
  • Wiring completion-related certification as required by BESCOM

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Choose the correct consumer category and load
  2. Apply through BESCOM's official channel with documents
  3. Support the site inspection
  4. Pay the published charges and complete metering

Fees, stamp duty and timelines

Government fee
Government fees, where applicable, are paid only on the official portal or at the government office. Confirm the current amount on the official portal before payment.
Assistance charges
Professional assistance charges are quoted after understanding your requirement. Government fees and SRCHN service charges are always shown separately. Confirm the final quote before work begins.
Processing time
Processing time varies by office and case. No completion timeline is guaranteed.
Government fees and SRCHN assistance charges are always kept separate. Nothing here is an invented price - confirm current amounts on the official portal or with our team before paying.
Legal requirements
  • Electricity Act, 2003 and the applicable supply code
Common rejection reasons
  • Premises wiring not compliant
  • Ownership or occupancy not established
  • Load requested inconsistent with the installation
Important cautions
Use only BESCOM's official channels and published charges
Keep the application acknowledgement for tracking

FAQs

How long does a new connection take?
Timelines depend on inspection and category. BESCOM publishes process details - no fixed timeline is promised here.
Can a tenant apply?
With the owner's consent and documents as BESCOM requires - confirm the current rules.
What load should I apply for?
Match the sanctioned load to the installation and intended use - an electrician's assessment helps.
