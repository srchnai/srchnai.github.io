Khatha Correction and e-Khatha Assistance

Correct names, addresses, dimensions and classification errors in Khatha and e-Khatha records.

Khatha and Municipal Property Recordse-Khatha assistanceAvailable - Phase 1

What is this service?

Correction of errors in the municipal property record.

Errors in the municipal record - misspelled names, wrong dimensions, wrong usage classification - block tax payments and transactions. Corrections are applied through the municipal system with supporting documents, and e-Khatha records sometimes need reconciliation with older manual records.

Why and when you need it

Why
Tax demands, sales and loans all read the Khatha - errors there surface at the worst time.
Who needs it
Owners whose Khatha or e-Khatha record carries errors.
When
As soon as an error is noticed, and always before a transaction.

Required documents

  • Khatha certificate and extract showing the error
  • Title documents supporting the correct particulars
  • Identity proof of the owner
  • Supporting records - tax receipts, approved plans, sale deed

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Identify each error against the supporting record
  2. Apply for correction on the municipal portal or office
  3. Produce supporting documents at verification
  4. Confirm the corrected certificate and extract

Legal requirements
  • Applicable Karnataka municipal law
Common rejection reasons
  • Supporting documents do not establish the correct particulars
  • The change sought is actually a transfer, not a correction
  • Conflicting records across manual and e-Khatha systems
Important cautions
A name change by sale or inheritance is a transfer, not a correction - use the transfer route
Verify the corrected record before relying on it in a transaction

FAQs

My name is spelled wrong on the Khatha - correction or transfer?
A spelling error is a correction; a different owner is a transfer.
e-Khatha shows different details from my old Khatha - which is right?
They need reconciliation through the municipal authority. Do not assume either is correct - apply with your title records.
Can dimensions be corrected?
Yes, with supporting survey or approval records.
