BDA Lease-cum-Sale Documentation

Documentation for BDA sites held on lease-cum-sale - instalments, possession and the final sale deed.

BDA and Planning Authority ServicesBDA lease-cum-sale documentationAvailable - Phase 1

What is this service?

Management of BDA lease-cum-sale records through to the final sale deed.

Many BDA allotments are made on a lease-cum-sale basis: the allottee pays in instalments and the absolute sale deed is executed only after the conditions are met and the lock-in period ends. Keeping the allotment, payment and possession records straight is essential to getting the final deed.

Why and when you need it

Why
Ownership is not absolute until the sale deed is executed after the lease-cum-sale conditions are met.
Who needs it
Allottees of BDA sites on lease-cum-sale terms.
When
From allotment through instalments to eligibility for the final sale deed.

Required documents

  • Allotment letter and lease-cum-sale agreement
  • Payment receipts for all instalments
  • Possession certificate or letter
  • Identity proof of the allottee
  • Current tax and Khatha records of the site

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Compile the allotment, agreement and payment records
  2. Confirm with BDA that the conditions and lock-in are satisfied
  3. Apply to BDA for execution of the sale deed
  4. Execute the sale deed and register it
  5. Update Khatha and tax records after registration

Legal requirements
  • BDA allotment and lease-cum-sale terms govern the conditions and lock-in
  • Registration Act, 1908 - the final sale deed is registered
Common rejection reasons
  • Instalment arrears or missing payment proof
  • Lock-in period not completed
Important cautions
Transfer during the lock-in period is restricted - confirm the current BDA position
Preserve every instalment receipt permanently

FAQs

When do I get the sale deed for my BDA site?
After the lease-cum-sale conditions and lock-in are satisfied, on application to BDA. The exact conditions are in your allotment documents.
