Property Purchase Documentation Assistance

One coordinated purchase file - verification, agreement, stamp duty guidance, registration and post-registration record updates.

Property PurchaseResidential property purchaseAvailable - Phase 1

What is this service?

Coordinated documentation support across the entire purchase.

Buying property is a chain of steps across verification, agreement, stamping, registration and record updates. This service coordinates the full documentation side of a purchase so each step happens in the right order with the right records - with the legal opinion coming from an independent qualified lawyer.

Why and when you need it

Why
The steps depend on each other - a miss early surfaces at registration or resale.
Who needs it
Buyers of residential and commercial property in Bengaluru.
When
From shortlisting to after registration.

Required documents

  • Seller's title and records for verification
  • Buyer identity documents
  • Agreed commercial terms

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Verify title and records
  2. Execute the agreement to sell
  3. Prepare and register the sale deed
  4. Update Khatha, tax and utility accounts

Fees, stamp duty and timelines

Government fee

Assistance charges

Processing time
Processing time varies by office and case. No completion timeline is guaranteed.
Government fees and SRCHN assistance charges are always kept separate. Nothing here is an invented price - confirm current amounts on the official portal or with our team before paying.
Legal requirements
  • Transfer of Property Act, 1882
  • Registration Act, 1908
  • Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957
Common rejection reasons
  • Verification skipped in a hurry
  • Post-registration record updates left undone
Important cautions
Do not pay large advances before verification
Update Khatha, tax and utilities promptly after registration
