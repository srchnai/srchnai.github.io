Foreign-Executed / NRI Power of Attorney

Authentication and adjudication route for Powers of Attorney executed outside India by NRIs and outstation principals.

Power Of AttorneyForeign Executed Power of AttorneyAvailable - Phase 1

What is this service?

The end-to-end route for making a foreign-signed PoA valid for use in Karnataka.

A PoA executed outside India must be authenticated as the law requires - typically before the Indian consulate or through apostille where applicable - and then adjudicated and stamped in Karnataka within the prescribed time before use. This service coordinates drafting, consular authentication guidance, adjudication and use in the intended transaction.

Why and when you need it

Why
A PoA signed abroad without the required authentication and stamping will be refused by the SRO and counterparties.
Who needs it
NRIs and principals residing abroad who must act through an attorney in India.
When
Before any transaction where the NRI principal cannot be present.

Required documents

  • Draft PoA with precise powers and property schedule
  • Passport and overseas address proof of the principal
  • Identity proof of the attorney in India
  • Ownership documents of the property

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Draft the PoA for the specific need
  2. Execute it abroad before the Indian consulate or through apostille as applicable
  3. Send the original to India
  4. Adjudicate and stamp it in Karnataka within the prescribed time
  5. Use it in the transaction, uploading it where required on Kaveri 2.0

Legal requirements
  • Powers-of-Attorney Act, 1882
  • Indian Stamp Act, 1899 and Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957 for instruments received in India
Common rejection reasons
  • Authentication by the wrong authority
  • Stamping in India missed or delayed beyond the prescribed period
  • Powers broader than the transaction justifies
Important cautions
Timelines for stamping foreign-executed instruments are strict - confirm the current rule before the document travels
Keep proof of the consular authentication with the instrument

FAQs

Is a notarised foreign PoA enough?
Usually not by itself. The law requires authentication by the specified authorities for use in India - confirm the route for the country of execution.
How long is a foreign PoA valid?
Until revoked or as stated in it - but stamping and use windows in India are time-sensitive. Confirm current rules.
Can the whole process be done without travel?
The execution happens where the principal is. The India-side adjudication and transaction can be coordinated without the principal travelling.
