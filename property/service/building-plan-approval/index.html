Building Plan Approval Assistance

Plan sanction coordination with the competent municipal or planning authority - drawings, documents, application and follow-up.

BBMP and Municipal ServicesBuilding plan approvalAvailable - Phase 1

What is this service?

Sanction of construction drawings by the competent authority before building.

Construction needs a sanctioned building plan from the competent authority - the city municipal authority, BDA or the local planning authority depending on jurisdiction. Licensed architects and engineers prepare and certify the drawings; this service coordinates documentation, application and follow-up.

Why and when you need it

Why
Building without a sanctioned plan risks demolition, penalties and an unsellable property.
Who needs it
Owners planning construction, extension or alteration.
When
Before any construction begins.

Required documents

  • Title documents and Khatha
  • Site plan and survey records
  • Proposed drawings by a licensed architect or engineer
  • Identity proof of the owner
  • NOCs where the location requires them

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Confirm the competent authority for the site
  2. Prepare drawings through a licensed professional
  3. Apply with the full document set and prescribed fees
  4. Respond to objections raised in scrutiny
  5. Obtain the sanctioned plan before starting work

Fees, stamp duty and timelines

Government fee
Government fees, where applicable, are paid only on the official portal or at the government office. Confirm the current amount on the official portal before payment.
Assistance charges
Professional assistance charges are quoted after understanding your requirement. Government fees and SRCHN service charges are always shown separately. Confirm the final quote before work begins.
Processing time
Processing time varies by office and case. No completion timeline is guaranteed.
Government fees and SRCHN assistance charges are always kept separate. Nothing here is an invented price - confirm current amounts on the official portal or with our team before paying.
Legal requirements
  • Applicable municipal and planning laws
  • Building bye-laws of the competent authority
Common rejection reasons
  • Setback, FAR or height violations in the proposal
  • Title or jurisdiction not established
  • Drawings not certified by licensed professionals
Important cautions
Do not start construction before sanction
Deviations from the sanctioned plan create occupancy and resale problems
Jurisdiction decides the authority - confirm it first

FAQs

Which authority sanctions my plan?
It depends on the property's jurisdiction - city municipal authority, BDA or a local planning authority. Confirm before applying.
Can I regularise later?
Regularisation schemes are limited and time-bound. Never build assuming regularisation will be available.
Who prepares the drawings?
Licensed architects and engineers. SRCHN coordinates; the licensed professional certifies.
