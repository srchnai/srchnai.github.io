e-Swathu Form 11A and 11B

Obtaining Form 11A / 11B - the gram panchayat demand-and-collection registers needed alongside Form 9.

Gram Panchayat and Rural Property ServicesForm 11AAvailable - Phase 1

What is this service?

SRCHN.PROPERTY retrieves Form 11A and 11B entries from e-Swathu for the required period, or applies with the panchayat where the records are manual, and assembles them with Form 9 into the set transactions expect.

Form 11A records the panchayat's tax demand and Form 11B the collection - together the rural equivalent of the urban Khatha extract and tax receipt. Banks and buyers typically ask for Form 9 with Form 11A/11B to confirm both assessment and payment status. Digitised entries are searchable on e-Swathu.

Why and when you need it

Why
Form 9 alone proves assessment but not payment. The 11A/11B pair is what actually satisfies lenders and buyers on tax status.
Who needs it
Owners and buyers of gram panchayat properties completing due diligence or loan documentation.
When
Alongside Form 9, before sale or loan processing.

Required documents

  • Property details (district, taluk, hobli, village, property number)
  • Form 9 (where available)
  • Owner ID proof
  • Period for which the records are needed

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Search the property on e-Swathu
  2. Retrieve Form 11A and Form 11B entries for the required period
  3. Apply with the panchayat for manual-era records
  4. Assemble the Form 9 + 11A + 11B set for the transaction
