Mortgage Release and Re-conveyance

Closing a mortgage after loan repayment - release deeds, re-conveyance and EC cleanup.

Mortgage, Bank and Finance DocumentationMortgage release

What is this service?

Registered closure of a mortgage and recovery of title documents.

After a loan is fully repaid, the mortgage must be formally closed: the lender returns the title deeds and, for registered mortgages, a release or re-conveyance deed is registered so the Encumbrance Certificate reflects a clear title.

Why and when you need it

Why
Until the release is registered, the mortgage continues to appear against the property.
Who needs it
Owners who have fully repaid a mortgage loan.
When
Immediately after loan closure and collection of originals from the lender.

Required documents

  • Loan closure letter and no-dues certificate from the lender
  • Original mortgage deed particulars
  • Title documents returned by the lender
  • Identity proofs of the parties
  • Latest tax paid receipts

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Collect the closure letter and original documents from the lender
  2. Draft the release or re-conveyance deed
  3. Enter details and upload the draft on Kaveri 2.0
  4. Pay applicable stamp duty and registration fees
  5. Register at the SRO and then verify the EC reflects the release

Legal requirements
  • Transfer of Property Act, 1882
  • Registration Act, 1908
  • Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957
Common rejection reasons
  • Closure letter or no-dues certificate not produced
  • Mortgage particulars not matching registration records
Important cautions
Verify the EC after registration to confirm the charge is cleared
Keep the lender's closure letter permanently with your title documents

FAQs

The bank returned my documents - is the mortgage closed?
For an equitable mortgage, returning the deeds generally closes it. For a registered mortgage, a release deed must also be registered.
