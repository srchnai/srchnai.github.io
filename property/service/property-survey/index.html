Property Survey and Boundary Demarcation

Licensed survey of land and buildings - measurement, boundary demarcation, survey sketches and discrepancy reports.

Survey, Land Measurement and MappingLand surveyAvailable - Phase 1

What is this service?

Professional measurement and demarcation of a property on the ground.

Survey work fixes what is actually on the ground against what records say - boundaries, measurements, encroachments and discrepancies. It is done through licensed surveyors and the survey department's records.

Why and when you need it

Why
Boundary and measurement mismatches are among the most common property disputes.
Who needs it
Buyers of plots and land, owners fencing or building, and parties in boundary disputes.
When
Before buying land, before construction, and when a boundary is questioned.

Required documents

  • Title documents with the property schedule
  • RTC and survey records for land
  • Village map or layout plan where available

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Collect the record schedule and maps
  2. Conduct the field survey with a licensed surveyor
  3. Compare ground position with records
  4. Deliver the sketch and discrepancy report

Legal requirements
  • Applicable land survey and settlement law
Common rejection reasons
  • Record schedule ambiguous or outdated
  • Neighbours disputing the boundary during demarcation
Important cautions
A private survey does not settle a dispute - it documents positions; disputes need the authority or courts
Encroachment findings should be acted on promptly with advice

FAQs

Who can survey my land?
Licensed surveyors; official demarcation goes through the survey department's process.
The ground and the deed do not match - what now?
A discrepancy report documents it. Correction of records or a dispute process may follow - take advice.
Is a survey needed for an apartment?
Less often - surveys matter most for plots, land and independent houses.
