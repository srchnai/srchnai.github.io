Piped Natural Gas (PNG) Connection Transfer

Transferring a PNG connection to the new owner or tenant with the city gas distributor.

Other Utility and Service TransfersPiped natural gas transferAvailable - Phase 1

What is this service?

SRCHN.PROPERTY identifies the distributor serving the property, prepares the transfer application with the new occupant's KYC and property documents, files it, and follows through until the account reflects the new name.

Piped natural gas connections in Bengaluru are provided by the city gas distributor for the area. On a change of occupancy the connection should be transferred to the new owner or tenant - with the KYC and ownership or tenancy documents - so billing and safety responsibility sit with the actual user.

Why and when you need it

Why
An untransferred PNG account leaves the previous occupant liable for consumption and safety compliance at a home they no longer control.
Who needs it
Buyers, sellers and tenants of homes with piped gas.
When
At handover of a home with a PNG connection.

Required documents

  • Existing customer / account details
  • New occupant's ID and address proof
  • Sale deed or rental agreement
  • Last gas bill

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Identify the city gas distributor for the area
  2. Prepare the transfer application with KYC and property documents
  3. File the application and complete any inspection
  4. Confirm the account is re-issued in the new occupant's name
