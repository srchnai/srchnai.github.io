Rental Agreement

Residential rental agreements with correct stamping, clear deposit, lock-in, maintenance and exit clauses.

Rental and LeasingRental agreements

What is this service?

The contract between landlord and tenant for occupation of a property.

A rental agreement records the rent, deposit, duration, lock-in, maintenance responsibilities, notice period and what happens on default. In Bengaluru, residential agreements are commonly made for eleven months and renewed, but the right structure depends on your tenancy - registration and stamping rules must be checked for your case, not assumed.

Why and when you need it

Why
It protects the deposit, fixes rent and notice, and is the first document asked for in any dispute.
Who needs it
Landlords and tenants of houses, apartments, villas and PG accommodation.
When
Before handing over possession or paying the deposit.

Required documents

  • Ownership proof of the landlord (title deed or Khatha)
  • Identity proofs of landlord and tenant
  • Property address and schedule
  • Agreed rent, deposit and terms

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Verify the landlord's ownership or authority to rent
  2. Fix rent, deposit, duration, lock-in, notice and maintenance terms
  3. Draft the agreement with schedules for furnishings and condition
  4. Stamp the agreement correctly and sign with witnesses
  5. Register where the law requires for the agreed term

Legal requirements
  • Karnataka Rent Act, 1999 and the Transfer of Property Act, 1882 as applicable
  • Registration Act, 1908 - leases beyond the exempted period require registration
  • Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957
Common rejection reasons
  • No lock-in or notice clause - disputes on early exit
  • Deposit refund terms not recorded
  • Landlord's ownership or authority not verified
Important cautions
Do not assume an 11-month agreement is universally exempt from registration - check the current law for your tenancy
Notarisation does not replace registration where registration is required
Record the condition of the property and furnishings in a schedule with photos

FAQs

Is an 11-month agreement always enough?
Eleven-month agreements are common practice, but the exemption from registration depends on the law applicable to your tenancy and term. Confirm for your case - do not assume.
Should the agreement be notarised or registered?
It depends on the term and the law. Notarisation authenticates signatures; registration is a separate legal requirement where applicable.
What deposit terms should be recorded?
Amount, what it secures, deductions allowed, interest if any, and the exact timeline and mode of refund on vacating.
