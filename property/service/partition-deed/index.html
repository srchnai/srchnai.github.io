Partition Deed

Documentation and registration of partition deeds dividing jointly held or inherited property among co-owners.

Deeds and ConveyancingPartition deedsAvailable - Phase 1

What is this service?

A registered instrument dividing joint property among co-owners.

A partition deed divides joint or inherited property among co-owners by metes and bounds or by defined shares. A registered partition deed gives each co-owner an independent, marketable title to their portion.

Why and when you need it

Why
It converts undivided shares into clearly demarcated individual ownership.
Who needs it
Co-owners, joint families and legal heirs dividing property.
When
When co-owners agree to divide the property, or to formalise a family arrangement.

Required documents

  • Title documents of the joint property
  • Legal heir or family records where the property is inherited
  • Khatha and tax records
  • Encumbrance Certificate
  • Proposed division plan or schedule of shares
  • Identity proofs of all co-owners

Identity and supporting documents

  • PAN card of each applicant or party
  • Aadhaar card or another government photo identity and address proof
  • Passport-size photographs
  • For companies and associations: incorporation documents, board resolution and authorisation letter

Step-by-step procedure

  1. Confirm all co-owners and their shares
  2. Prepare a division plan with survey details where portions are physical
  3. Draft the partition deed recording each party's allotment
  4. Pay applicable stamp duty and register at the SRO
  5. Update Khatha, tax and revenue records for each portion

Fees, stamp duty and timelines

Stamp duty
Stamp duty and registration charges are set by the Department of Stamps and Registration, Government of Karnataka, and depend on the instrument and the property value. Confirm the current amount on the official Kaveri / IGR portal before paying anything.
Government fee
Government fees, where applicable, are paid only on the official portal or at the government office. Confirm the current amount on the official portal before payment.
Assistance charges
Professional assistance charges are quoted after understanding your requirement. Government fees and SRCHN service charges are always shown separately. Confirm the final quote before work begins.
Processing time
Processing time varies by office and case. No completion timeline is guaranteed.
Government fees and SRCHN assistance charges are always kept separate. Nothing here is an invented price - confirm current amounts on the official portal or with our team before paying.
Legal requirements
  • Registration Act, 1908
  • Karnataka Stamp Act, 1957
  • Applicable succession law for inherited property
Common rejection reasons
  • All co-owners or legal heirs not joined
  • Disputed shares or pending litigation not disclosed
  • Physical division inconsistent with survey records
Important cautions
Every person with a share should be a party - leaving out an heir invalidates the partition against them
A family arrangement and a partition deed have different legal treatment - choose with advice
After registration, mutate Khatha and revenue records for each portion

FAQs

Can property be partitioned orally?
A family arrangement may be recognised in limited cases, but a registered deed is the reliable record. Without it, each co-owner's title stays undivided and harder to transact.
Do all heirs need to sign?
Yes, every person entitled to a share should execute the deed or be properly represented.
Is stamp duty payable on partition?
Yes, partition deeds attract stamp duty under the Karnataka Stamp Act. Confirm the current amount on the official portal.
