How jurisdiction actually works here

City properties

Most urban Bengaluru addresses: Greater Bengaluru Authority (formerly BBMP) for Khatha and property tax, BDA for planning and layout records, SRO for registrations.

Fringe and rural properties

Outskirts can sit under gram panchayats (records on e-Swathu) or other municipal bodies, with land records on Bhoomi. Check before you assume.

Verification portals: e-Aasthi (GBA/BBMP property records) · e-Swathu (gram panchayat records) · Bhoomi (revenue records) · Kaveri 2.0 (registration). Full details in the government directory.
