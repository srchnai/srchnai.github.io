SRCHN BRANDS

Interface demos.

Three mockups of what is coming to SRCHN Brands: SRCHN PAY checkout for plans and payments, DigiLocker verification for brand owners, and DigiYatra-style face verification. They are demonstrations only - no real payment, no real verification, and nothing you do here leaves your device.

SRCHN PAY

Checkout and wallet flow - order summary, UPI / card / wallet, processing and receipt. Configuration required, currently disabled: no real payment is ever taken today; brand plans are activated by Team SRCHN against confirmed payment references.

DIGILOCKER · OWNER VERIFICATION

Consent-first verification of a brand owner's name and date of birth through the official DigiLocker flow - built for claiming and managing a brand. Configuration required, currently disabled. SRCHN never asks for your DigiLocker password or OTP and never collects your Aadhaar number.

FACE VERIFICATION

A DigiYatra-style, app-like liveness check - center, turn, blink, done. Demo only: no photo or video is captured, stored or uploaded, and no real face matching runs. SRCHN is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, DigiLocker, UIDAI or DigiYatra.
