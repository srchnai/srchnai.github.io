Privacy

This page describes this preview only. It is plain-language information, not legal advice.

What this site stores

  • Saved items in the feed and Studio demo settings stay in your browser (local storage). SRCHN does not receive them. Clearing site data removes them.
  • The main pages (home, feed, places and Studio) have no accounts, no ads and no tracking scripts.
  • Place submissions are switched off. If they are switched on, the form says what it collects and how long it is kept.

What others may see

  • Our host, Cloudflare, handles each request and may keep standard request logs under its own policies.
  • Map links open OpenStreetMap, a separate site with its own policy.
  • Your Local Buddy is a separate preview app with its own privacy page.
