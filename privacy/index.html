Privacy

This page describes this preview only. It is plain-language information, not legal advice, and it is not presented as a final legal publication.

What this site stores

  • Saved items in the feed, Make a Plan and Studio demo settings stay in your browser (local storage). SRCHN does not receive them. Clearing site data removes them.
  • The separate signed-in pilot saved-cafes profile stores saved cafes server-side for that signed-in account. Browser-local saved places are separate, and the profile page says which one you are using.
  • The request-for-review page saves only the request described on that page for SRCHN review. It is not sent to a venue or club and does not create a booking, membership or ticket. Its own notice gives the current retention period and deletion route.
  • Place submissions are switched off. While paused, nothing entered on that form is saved. If submissions reopen, the form says what it collects, how long it is kept and how to request deletion.
  • Home, feed, place pages and Studio have no public accounts, ads or SRCHN analytics trackers.

Club preview

  • SRCHN CLUB is a product preview. It does not charge money, complete bookings, issue entry passes, activate membership or pay cashback.
  • The identity journey is a designed flow only. No Aadhaar number, identity document, biometric sample, face scan or liveness data is collected or stored here.
  • Email login is limited to the preview sign-in journey. It does not activate membership, payments, bookings or a digital pass.

Studio, media and content rights

  • The Studio photo-to-video tool processes the selected photo in your browser. The photo and generated video are not uploaded by that tool.
  • Studio asks you to confirm that you own the photo or have permission to use it. Credit, source and licence fields stay with the local export; SRCHN does not check them automatically.
  • Publishing, creator earnings, subscriptions, takedown cases and reviewer workflows are planned or simulated only, as labelled on the page.

Business claims and verification

  • Claiming a place starts a manual review request by email. Owner verification and business tooling are not live in this preview.
  • A submitted claim does not make a business verified. No business should be shown as verified without an actual completed verification process.

What others may see

  • Our host, Cloudflare, handles each request and may keep standard request logs under its own policies.
  • Map links open OpenStreetMap, a separate site with its own policy. Map tiles contact OpenStreetMap only after you choose to load the interactive map.
  • Your Local Buddy is a separate preview app with its own privacy page.

Cookies, analytics, complaints and deletion

  • No cookie banner is shown because this preview does not intentionally use non-essential cookies. Browser local storage is used only for the features described above.
  • To remove browser-local items, clear this site's data in your browser. Remove pilot saved cafes from the signed-in pilot profile. Use a request receipt or the reference shown by a submission form where one exists.
  • Questions, corrections, complaints or removal requests: hi@srchn.in.
  • Formal Terms of Use, a final cookie policy, a final accessibility statement and production data-processing terms remain subject to legal review before launch.
