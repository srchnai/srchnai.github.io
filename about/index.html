About SRCHN

SEE IT BEFORE YOU GO. SRCHN is a preview of a visual guide to Bengaluru's places, spaces and experiences.

What works in this preview

  • Browsing ten categories: Live, Work, Eat, Drink, Play, Shop, Travel, Events, Movies and Community.
  • Open-data place lists with names and map points from OpenStreetMap, each with a detail page.
  • Your Local Buddy and a Studio workspace. The Studio can make short preview videos in your browser.

What is not live

  • Place and listing data are raw map features. Current opening, hours, fees and booking are unknown.
  • Sample photos illustrate categories. They are not current listings. Only two museums have a photo of the actual place.
  • Creator, brand approval, coins, plans and similar Studio items are demonstrations, not working services.
  • Place submissions are switched off. Nothing is published automatically.

Photos and place data

Photos show Bengaluru places and experiences, not current listings or availability. The Aero India image is from 2025; the cinema image is from 2018. Photo sources and licences: Credits. Place data: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL) and Overture Maps.
