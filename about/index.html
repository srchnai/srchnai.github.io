SRCHN

The AI Operating System for a City.

Powered by AI. Experienced by Humans.

Cities are full of possibilities. Thousands of places to discover, businesses to explore, experiences to enjoy and people to connect with. Yet, finding the right things and making the most of everything a city offers often remains fragmented across multiple platforms.

We believe there should be a better way.

SRCHN is building an AI Operating System for Cities, an intelligent, interconnected digital ecosystem that brings people, businesses, places and experiences together through artificial intelligence, automation, location intelligence and digital transactions.

Starting with Bengaluru, our vision is to simplify how people discover, experience and engage with their city.

More than discovery. A connected city experience.

SRCHN brings together multiple technology capabilities under one ecosystem:

  • DISCOVER: Explore places, businesses, neighbourhoods and experiences through video-first, hyperlocal discovery.
  • STUDIO: Enable businesses to create engaging digital content through AI-assisted content creation and automation.
  • CLUB: Connect people with experiences, communities, venues and rewards.
  • PAY: Enable seamless digital payment and transaction experiences through integrated financial technology.

Each capability contributes to a larger vision: making the digital and physical worlds work better together.

Technology powered by AI. Experiences centred on people.

Artificial intelligence powers the intelligence, automation and technology behind SRCHN. But technology is never the destination.

People are.

We believe AI should make everyday experiences simpler, more accessible, more relevant and more meaningful — without taking away the human connections that make a city special.

The system is powered through AI. The experience is human.

Starting with one city. Thinking beyond cities.

Bengaluru is where we begin.

A city known for its entrepreneurial spirit, technology, culture, communities and endless possibilities.

Our ambition is to build a scalable technology platform that can understand the unique character of each city and connect its people, businesses and experiences through one intelligent ecosystem.

We are not building another city guide.

We are building the intelligence and automation layer that connects a city.
