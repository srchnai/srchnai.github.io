Interface demos.

These three mockups show how SRCHN PAY, DigiLocker verification and DigiYatra-style face verification will feel once their integrations are approved and configured. They are demonstrations only - no real payment, no real verification, and nothing you do here leaves your device.

SRCHN PAY

Checkout and wallet flow - order summary, UPI / card / wallet, processing and receipt. Configuration required, currently disabled: no real payment is ever taken today.

DIGILOCKER · AADHAAR VERIFICATION

Consent-first identity verification of name and date of birth through the official DigiLocker flow. Configuration required, currently disabled. SRCHN never asks for your DigiLocker password or OTP and never collects your Aadhaar number.

FACE VERIFICATION

A DigiYatra-style, app-like liveness check - center, turn, blink, done. Demo only: no photo or video is captured, stored or uploaded, and no real face matching runs. SRCHN is not affiliated with, or endorsed by, DigiLocker, UIDAI or DigiYatra.
